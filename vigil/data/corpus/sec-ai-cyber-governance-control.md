# SEC AI and Cyber Governance Control

## AI model governance

Financial services AI systems used for customer-facing workflows, credit review, or
regulated reporting must have documented model purpose, approved use cases, risk
assessment, assigned business owner, and evidence of compliance review before launch.

## Cyber incident escalation

Material cybersecurity incidents, AI system failures, or control breakdowns that may
affect customers, regulated reporting, or investor disclosures must be escalated to
Legal, Security, and Compliance within one business day with supporting logs.

## Vendor oversight

Critical vendors that provide AI systems, model services, customer support automation,
or cybersecurity tooling must provide security addendum evidence, incident notification
terms, annual assurance review materials, and remediation owner contacts.

## Audit evidence

Control owners must retain AI governance approvals, model monitoring evidence,
cybersecurity incident records, vendor assurance materials, and remediation decisions
for audit, regulatory examination, or disclosure review.
