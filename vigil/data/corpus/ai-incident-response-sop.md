# AI Incident Response SOP

## Escalation

Material AI incidents must be escalated to Compliance and Security within one business
day with supporting logs. Incidents involving regulated users, automated decisions, or
high-risk AI workflows require Legal review before external notification.

## Evidence collection

Incident responders must preserve prompt logs, model outputs, system configuration,
affected user segments, human reviewer notes, and remediation decisions.

## Closure

Closure requires a root-cause summary, control update recommendation, and approval from
the incident owner and compliance reviewer.
