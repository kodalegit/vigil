# Model Risk Control Register

## Monitoring cadence

High-impact models require quarterly monitoring, named reviewers, and retained evidence
of validation results. Monitoring must include drift checks, performance thresholds,
and review of exception queues.

## Incident logging

Material model failures, unexpected harmful outputs, or performance degradation must be
logged with severity, affected product, timestamp, and remediation owner. Incidents that
could create material customer, compliance, or safety risk must be escalated to Legal,
Security, and Compliance.

## Audit evidence

Control owners must keep validation reports, monitoring dashboards, test data summaries,
approval evidence, and remediation notes for regulator or auditor review.
