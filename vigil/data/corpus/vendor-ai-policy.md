# Vendor AI Policy

## Vendor due diligence

Vendors that provide AI systems for customer-facing workflows must disclose model purpose,
training data categories, evaluation procedures, and known limitations before contract
approval.

## Contract controls

Contracts for high-impact AI vendors must include audit assistance, incident notification,
data protection terms, and evidence retention obligations.

## Ongoing monitoring

Vendor owners must review AI vendor performance, incident reports, and compliance
attestations at least annually.
