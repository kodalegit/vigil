# AI System Inventory

## Customer support triage model

Classifies customer support requests and routes urgent complaints to operations teams.
The system is medium impact and has a human review queue for escalations.

## Credit risk assistant

Assists analysts reviewing business credit applications. The system is high impact,
requires human decision review, and stores validation reports in the model risk folder.

## Sales content assistant

Generates draft sales emails and account summaries. The system is low impact and must not
make eligibility, pricing, or risk decisions.
