# AI Governance Policy

## Production approval

Product teams must document model purpose, approved use cases, assigned business owner,
and model limitations before production launch. High-impact AI systems require a named
human reviewer and a documented escalation path before release.

## Human oversight

Every high-risk or high-impact AI workflow must include a documented human oversight
procedure. The procedure must describe when a human can intervene, override, pause,
or stop system output and who is accountable for review.

## Evidence records

AI system owners must retain approval records, review notes, and evidence of control
checks for audit and compliance review.
