# AI Data Protection Impact Assessment Template

## Processing context

Teams must describe the AI use case, data categories, affected users, automated decision
logic, and jurisdictional scope.

## Risk assessment

The assessment must document privacy, bias, safety, and compliance risks, including any
high-risk AI classification or regulated sector obligations.

## Mitigation and sign-off

Controls must include human review, appeal paths, monitoring, retention limits, and final
approval from Privacy and Compliance.
